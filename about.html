A neighborhood, not a facility.

Twin Villas is an independently owned senior living community. Here's what we're trying to build and why.

Our story

Twin Villas started with a simple frustration: most senior living looked like a hotel hallway, not a neighborhood. So we built villas in pairs instead of long buildings, gave each resident their own front door, and kept the whole community small enough that the staff actually know your name.

We're locally owned and operated, not part of a national chain, which means the person who answers the phone is the same person who can make a decision.

Growing older should mean fewer chores, not less independence. Twin Villas exists to give residents a home of their own, a neighbor close by, and a team that quietly takes care of the rest.

What we hold ourselves to

These are the standards we measure ourselves against, from the first tour to the day-to-day of living here.

Independence first

Every decision starts with what keeps residents living life on their own terms, in their own villa.

Real neighbors

Villas are paired, not stacked into hallways, so community happens over a fence line, not on a schedule.

Straightforward answers

We tell you plainly what we do and don't provide, so you and your family can plan with confidence.

Handled without a hassle

Maintenance requests get a fast answer and a real fix, so your time stays yours, not spent chasing repairs.
