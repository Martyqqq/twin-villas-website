Everything nearby, without needing a car.

Twin Villas sits in a quiet residential pocket of Fort Wayne, minutes from the places residents visit most.

Find us

2132 West Till Road, Fort Wayne, IN 46818 — convenient access to Lima Road.

What's close by

A short drive puts residents near healthcare, parks, and everyday errands.

Rivergreen Park

Paved walking trails along the river, with benches every quarter mile.

0.8 miles away

Parkview Regional Hospital

Full-service hospital and urgent care, with a shuttle stop at the clubhouse.

2.1 miles away

Orchard Square Shopping

Grocery, pharmacy, a post office, and a handful of local restaurants.

1.4 miles away

Getting around

Residents will require transportation for errands and off-site activities.

  • Bring your own car

    Every villa includes a private one-car garage. There is no sidewalk access to or on the main roads nearby.
